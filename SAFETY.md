# Safety overview

Companion assets are **practice aids**. Treat every template example as fictional unless **you** replace it with sanitized, policy-approved material.

## Rules

- **No secrets**, credentials, production logs, customer identifiers, or privileged incident detail.
- **Defensive posture only**—no exploit steps, malware, phishing kits, bypass guidance, persistence, privilege escalation, or offensive-target instructions.
- **Validate outputs** before relying on them; models and tools drift.
- **Human review** belongs before outward **`STATE_CHANGE`**, regulated commitments, or operational sends.

Full companion security posture notes live in **`COMPANION-SECURITY-REVIEW.md`** when present.
