## What this is

Private **Markdown-first** workbook material: reusable **POP**-shaped templates, fictional examples, checklist scaffolds, and workflow shells that reinforce ideas from the book. This repo is **not** a substitute for reading the manuscript and **does not** ship finished book prose.

## How it relates to the book

Readers use these files **while** practising **prompt blocks**, **chains**, **contracts**, **validation**, **libraries**, **STOP_CONDITIONS**, and **human-review gates** described in the book. **`BOOK-COMPANION-NOTES.md`** maps manuscript chapters to files here.

## What is included

- **`/templates`** — blanks for prompts, chains, STOP_CONDITIONS, tool permissions, state-change records, library entries, agent-loop specs  
- **`/workflows`** — draft procedural shells matching example workflow arcs in the book (fictional data only)  
- **`/examples`** — short fictional vignettes (**Jordan Lee**, **Harbor Trail Coffee**, **Northstar Publishing**, etc.) — **not real people or companies**  
- **`/checklists`** — reader safety / POP reinforcement lists  
- **`/fixtures`** — tiny sanitized stubs for practising redaction discipline  
- **`/docs`** — how to use this companion and terminology reminders  

## What is intentionally excluded

- **Final or draft manuscript** chapters  
- **KDP storefront files**, uploads, dashboards, binaries  
- **`*.epub`**, **`*.docx`**, **`*.pdf`** book exports (`/.gitignore` blocks them)  
- **Identifiable client or customer records**, privileged access material, internal runbooks  
- **Statistics, benchmarks, citations, or legal interpretations** invented for this workbook  

## Safety rules

1. Companion materials are **practice aids**, not authoritative sources or compliance advice.  
2. Examples are **fictional or sanitized**. Do **not** paste proprietary or regulated information.  
3. **Tool output is an artifact**, not ground truth until you validate it.  
4. **No outbound action that changes systems, customers, finances, HR, publishing, incidents, legal posture, medical decisions, security controls, external records**, or similarly high-impact domains may be described as proceeding **without explicit human approval** in your real workflow. Templates here reinforce that stance.
